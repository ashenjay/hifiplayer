🎧 HiFi Player 1.3 — a FREE bit-perfect music player for Windows

I've been building a free, open-source audiophile player for Windows, and version 1.3 is out!

✅ 100% bit-perfect: ASIO and WASAPI Exclusive, no mixer, no volume tricks
✅ Native DSD (DSD64–DSD512), DoP, DST and SACD ISO
✅ Spotify Connect — play from your phone, through your DAC
✅ AirPlay — iPhone / iPad / Mac straight to your DAC, lossless
✅ Lossless internet radio — Radio Paradise FLAC and many more
✅ Synced lyrics, TV mode, live spectrum, waveform seek bar
✅ Phone remote + a full-screen "Now Playing" page for a tablet
✅ Your library: playlists, favourites, cover art, artist bios, DR / fake hi-res check

No ads, no account, no subscription. Free for everyone (GPL-3.0).

📥 Download: https://github.com/ashenjay/hifiplayer/releases

Feedback and bug reports are very welcome! 🙏
